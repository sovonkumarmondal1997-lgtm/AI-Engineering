## Unit 10 — Common algebra mistakes
- **L1:** **the ten most common slips, each as a *wrong rule*, a *numeric counterexample*, and the *correct rule*:** (1) `(a + b)² = a² + b²`; (2) cancelling terms instead of factors; (3) combining unlike terms (`3x + 2y = 5xy`); (4) sign errors with a leading minus (`−(x − 4) = −x − 4`); (5) distributing to only the first term (`2(x + 3) = 2x + 3`); (6) `−x²` vs `(−x)²`; (7) adding fractions by adding tops and bottoms (M02 recall); (8) dividing by something that may be zero; (9) "`less than`" reversed; (10) the equals sign used as "then compute" (`2 + 3 = 5 + 4 = 9`). **Each counterexample is a specific exact numeric instance verified by code.**
