**Design decisions to state in Unit 0 and Unit 8 (deliberate, `JUDGEMENT`):**

1. **Embeddings are evaluated against a planted ground truth, offline.** The repository forbids downloaded corpora and pretrained vectors. **The default, graded corpora come from `lexgen`, a seeded generator of text whose semantic structure is known by construction** (word classes, synonym sets, planted analogy relations, a polysemous word, Zipfian frequencies, and a planted association "bias direction" between abstract groups), **and `sentgen`, a seeded generator of sentences with known paraphrase groups, graded similarity, negations, and role swaps** (Section 9). The ground-truth similarity is defined by the generator's true context distributions, computed **two independent ways** (exact enumeration where feasible and large-sample counting). **An optional "bring your own text" mode** (the learner's own writing, ≤ 1 MB, with a data-provenance note) is allowed for qualitative exploration only — no ground truth exists, and it is never graded. No pretrained embeddings are downloaded or used.
2. **A sentence encoder without transformers.** Attention arrives in Module 07, so **the module's sentence encoders are built from what the learner already has — pooled word embeddings, an MLP, and optionally the Module 05 recurrent or Module 04 convolutional layers** — and trained contrastively. The contrast with transformer encoders is a forward pointer (`../07-Attention-and-the-Transformer/`, `../14-Multimodal-Models/02-clip-and-image-text-alignment.md`).

**Priority:** Unit 2 (word2vec — the mathematics and the evaluation), Unit 3 (contrastive learning), and Unit 8 (the project) get the deepest treatment; Units 1, 4, 5 must be complete and rigorous; Units 0, 6, 7 meet their stated requirements.

## Conventions stated once, used everywhere (state them in Unit 0, apply in all units)

- **Embedding and similarity conventions (a table, stated once; mixing is a defect).** An embedding table is **`E: (V, d)` with row `i` the vector of item `i`**; skip-gram uses **two tables** — a **center ("input") table `W: (V, d)` and a context ("output") table `U: (V, d)`** — and **the vector used for evaluation is stated (`W`, or `W + U`, or the average) and tested as a hyperparameter**; **index `0` is reserved for padding/unknown and never evaluated**; **cosine similarity is computed on row-normalised vectors, a zero vector yields similarity `0` with a documented warning**, and **dot product and Euclidean distance are used only where stated, with their norm sensitivity measured**; similarity matrices are `(n, n)` with the diagonal excluded from rankings. **Out-of-vocabulary evaluation items are never silently dropped: the number excluded is reported with the metric.**
- **Corpus and vocabulary conventions.** A corpus is a list of **documents** (the unit of independence for splitting); tokens are whitespace "words" in this module (subwords are Module 08); **the vocabulary, the frequency table, the subsampling and negative-sampling distributions are built from the training documents only**; `min_count` pruning is applied **before** evaluation-set construction and the evaluation items that survive are reported. **Sentence-level data are split by paraphrase group (the generator's `frame_id`), never by sentence**, so that a paraphrase of a training sentence never appears in the evaluation set.
- **Planted-truth convention.** Every generator ships a **manifest of the planted truth** (word classes; synonym sets; the analogy relations and their pairs; the polysemous words and their two contexts; the **true context distributions** and the **ground-truth similarity matrix** derived from them; the planted bias direction and its true effect size; for sentences, the frames, paraphrase groups, graded similarity, negation and role-swap pairs) computed **two independent ways** (exact enumeration for the small variant and a large-sample empirical estimate with an interval). **A metric better than the planted ceiling means a bug or a leak.**
- **Evaluation-unit convention.** Word-level metrics (Spearman correlation with the planted similarity, analogy accuracy, neighbour precision, cluster recovery) have the **word** as the unit of independence — pairs sharing a word are not independent — so **intervals are computed by a bootstrap over words (cluster bootstrap), never over pairs**; sentence-level retrieval metrics use the **frame** (paraphrase group) as the unit. **Metrics that are not sums over examples are recomputed on each resample.**
- **Stability convention.** Every embedding method is trained with **≥ 10 seeds (`--quick` ≥ 5)** for word embeddings and **≥ 5 (`--quick` ≥ 3)** for neural sentence encoders and autoencoders; **embedding *stability* is a reported metric**: the mean overlap of each word's top-`k` neighbours across seeds (and the Procrustes-aligned distance, named), with intervals; **a single-seed neighbour list is never presented as a result**. Comparisons between methods obey the **fair-comparison protocol of Module 05** (equal tuning budgets, equal seed counts, **disjoint tuning and reporting seeds**, paired effect sizes with intervals, trials reported; link `../05-Sequence-Models-and-Tabular-Deep-Learning/README.md`, whose content is not yet authored — mark `OPEN` until reconciled) and **no method-family ranking is ever stated**.
- **Representation-evaluation convention.** A representation is evaluated **by what it is for**: (i) **intrinsic** (similarity correlation, analogy, cluster recovery, neighbour precision against planted truth), (ii) **extrinsic** (a linear probe or k-NN on a downstream task with a train/test split by the unit of independence), (iii) **retrieval** (recall@k and MRR with hard negatives), (iv) **geometry** (anisotropy: mean pairwise cosine; the singular-value spectrum / effective rank; alignment and uniformity), and (v) **cost** (dimension, memory, throughput). **Reconstruction error alone is never reported as representation quality.** **Two-dimensional projections (PCA, t-SNE, UMAP) are illustrations with a numeric neighbour-preservation score, never evidence** (link `5B-05 05-leakage-and-visualization-limitations.md`).
- **Bias-and-privacy convention.** **Association bias is measured on the planted abstract groups only** (an effect-size statistic with a permutation test, its limits stated), **any mitigation is evaluated on held-out association tests *and* on retained utility, and the claim is bounded to "reduces this measured association on these tests"** (never "debiased"); **privacy statements are bounded the same way** (embeddings can retain information about their inputs — a measured reconstruction/probing demonstration on the planted data; no anonymisation claim).
- **Inherited protocols.** The **5C training-run card** (sanity gates; ≥ 5 seeds; compute ledger; split hygiene; numerics record; claims bounded to scale — `../00-Stage-Overview/README.md`, content not yet authored, mark `OPEN` until reconciled), **Module 03's `Trainer`, step-accounting, loss-normalisation, logging-schema, and tuning/reporting-seed-disjointness conventions**, **Module 02's tensor card, equivalence-test, measurement, reproducibility, and security conventions**, and **Module 01's gradient-check and numerics conventions** apply unchanged and are linked, not restated at length. **Sanity gates adapted to embeddings:** the initial loss of the chosen objective is derived (for SGNS with `K` negatives and near-zero vectors, `(K + 1) ln 2`; for InfoNCE with `N` candidates, `ln N`); one batch can be overfit; the analytic gradient equals finite differences; the lookup equals the one-hot matrix product.
- **Measurement convention (inherited):** warm-up discarded, repeats with spread, injected clocks in tests, no exact timing assertions. **You cannot execute code: every timing, similarity score, accuracy, recall, or training-result figure is `[ILLUSTRATIVE]` with the command that produces the real value; parameter counts, FLOPs, closed-form quantities (initial losses, entropy and PMI values for the planted distributions, collision probabilities, projection errors), and hand-computed values are exact and may be stated.**
