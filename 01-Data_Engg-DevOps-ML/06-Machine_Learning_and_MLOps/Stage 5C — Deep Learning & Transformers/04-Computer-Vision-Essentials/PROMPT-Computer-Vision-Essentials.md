## Conventions stated once, used everywhere (state them in Unit 0, apply in all units)

- **Image, box, and mask conventions (a table, stated once; mixing is a defect).** Image batches are `(B, C, H, W)` (**NCHW**), `float32` in `[0, 1]` before normalisation; **normalisation statistics are computed on training images only and recorded with the model**; labels for classification are `int64` `(B,)`. **Boxes are `(x1, y1, x2, y2)` in absolute pixel units as floats with `x2 > x1`, `y2 > y1`, area `(x2 − x1)(y2 − y1)` (the continuous convention; the VOC-era "+1 pixel" convention is named as a source of off-by-one disagreements `[VERIFY]` and demonstrated, never mixed in)**; conversions to and from `xywh` and normalised formats are explicit functions with round-trip tests. **Semantic masks are `(B, H, W)` `int64` class indices** (a documented ignore index); instance masks are `(N, H, W)` `bool`; run-length encoding is a documented, tested round trip. **The origin is the top-left pixel; `x` is the column axis and `y` the row axis** (stated because it is the most common transposition bug).
