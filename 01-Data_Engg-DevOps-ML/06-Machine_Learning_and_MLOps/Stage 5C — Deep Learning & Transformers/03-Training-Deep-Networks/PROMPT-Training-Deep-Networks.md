- **Step-accounting convention (stated once; mixing is a defect).** Four distinct counters, always named: **micro-step** (one forward/backward on one micro-batch), **optimiser step** (one parameter update), **epoch** (one pass over the training set), and **examples seen** (or **tokens seen** for sequence tasks). **The budget of a run is stated in examples/tokens seen and optimiser steps, never in "epochs" alone.** Learning-rate schedules are functions of the **optimiser step**, and the schedule's total length is declared before the run.
- **Loss-normalisation convention.** The per-example loss is the mean over the batch unless stated; for variable-length sequences the **token-weighted** loss (sum of token losses divided by the total number of tokens in the effective batch) is the default and the choice is stated; **any aggregation across micro-batches or across validation batches is weighted by example/token counts, never an unweighted mean of means** (tested).
