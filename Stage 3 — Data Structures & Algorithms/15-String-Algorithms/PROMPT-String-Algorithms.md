## Unit 2 — KMP and the prefix function
- **L1:** **Knuth–Morris–Pratt (KMP)** avoids re-examining text characters after a mismatch by using what the matched part reveals about the pattern; the **prefix function** `π` of a string `s`: **`π[i]` = the length of the longest *proper* prefix of `s[0:i+1]` that is also a suffix of `s[0:i+1]` (its longest border)**; computed by hand for `"abacabab"` with the borders drawn; **using `π` to match**: scan the text keeping `k` = length of the current partial match; on mismatch fall back `k = π[k − 1]`; hand-run on a short text showing `k` after each character; **0-based convention stated** (`π[0] = 0`), with the **1-based "failure function" shown only as a contrast**.
- **L2:** **implementation of `prefix_function(s)`** in `O(n)` and **`kmp_find_all(text, pattern)`** **without concatenating a separator** (the matcher keeps its own `k` and, upon `k == m`, reports `i − m + 1` and sets `k = π[m − 1]` to continue **with overlaps**) — plus the **classic concatenation form `pattern + sep + text`** shown **with its failure demonstration when `sep` occurs in the data** (and the **safe alternative: tokens as lists with a unique sentinel object `object()`**); **applications** (each with algorithm card, oracle, tests): **all borders of a string** (follow the chain `π[n − 1], π[π[n − 1] − 1], …`), **smallest period** (`n − π[n − 1]`) and **whether the string is a repetition of a shorter block** (`n % p == 0` and `p < n`; equivalence with `(s + s).find(s, 1) < n`), **shortest palindrome obtainable by prepending characters** (prefix function of `s + sep + reverse(s)` — **or the separator-free formulation by running the matcher of `reverse(s)` against `s`** — implemented and compared), **longest prefix that is also a suffix ("longest happy prefix")**, **number of occurrences of each prefix inside the string** (counting with `π`: `cnt[π[i] − 1] += 1`, propagate from the end — **derived and verified**), **periodicity of every prefix** (the smallest period of each prefix from `π`), **string compression/run detection** (maximal repetitions named), **KMP automaton** (**a full transition table `δ[state][char]` built in `O(mσ)` from `π`; the matcher then does exactly one table lookup per character; `dict`-based rows for large alphabets**), **streaming matching** (state `k` persists across chunks — **chunk-split invariance tested at every split point**), **counting/locating occurrences in a stream of unknown length**, **searching in token lists** (words instead of characters).
- **L3:** **proofs written in full**: (a) **correctness of `π` computation**: **invariant — at the start of iteration `i`, `k = π[i − 1]`; the borders of `s[0:i]`'s extension are obtained from the borders of `s[0:i−1]` by trying `k, π[k − 1], …` in decreasing order**; the **lemma: the borders of `s[0:j]` are exactly `π[j − 1]` together with the borders of `s[0:π[j − 1]]`** (**proof by showing any border of `s[0:j]` shorter than the longest is a border of the longest border**); (b) **linear time by a potential argument**: **`k` increases by at most 1 per iteration (`≤ n` total increments) and every fallback strictly decreases `k` and `k ≥ 0`, so total fallbacks `≤ n` — `O(n)` total — proof**; (c) **KMP matching correctness** (**invariant: after reading `T[0:i+1]`, `k` equals the length of the longest suffix of `T[0:i+1]` that is a prefix of `P` (with `k < m` after the reset on a full match) — induction using the border lemma**); **time `O(n + m)`** (same potential argument; **at most `2n` character comparisons in the matching phase — derivation**); (d) **period theorem: the smallest period of `s` is `n − π[n − 1]`** (**border–period duality from Unit 1; the longest border gives the smallest period — proof**); **repetition criterion** (`s` is a power of a shorter string iff `p = n − π[n − 1]` divides `n` and `p < n` — **proof; counterexample for the non-dividing case**); (e) **Fine and Wilf's periodicity theorem** named (**if a string has periods `p` and `q` and length `≥ p + q − gcd(p, q)`, it has period `gcd(p, q)` — cited `[VERIFY]`; verified exhaustively on small binary strings**);
