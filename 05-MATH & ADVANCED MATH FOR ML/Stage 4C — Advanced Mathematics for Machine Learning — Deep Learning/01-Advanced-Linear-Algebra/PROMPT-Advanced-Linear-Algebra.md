- **L2:** **the two classical derivations** (the learner sees both and why they agree): **(A) maximum variance**: maximise `wᵀSw` subject to `‖w‖ = 1` — solved by the **Lagrange multiplier / Rayleigh-quotient** argument (Units 6 and `../03-…` link) giving `Sw = λw`; **(B) minimum reconstruction error**: minimise `Σ‖xᵢ − WWᵀxᵢ‖²` over orthonormal `W` — via projection (Unit 4); **explained variance and its cumulative sum**; **choosing `k`** (threshold, scree, error budget, cross-validated downstream task — each `HEURISTIC` with a failure mode); **standardisation vs covariance PCA** (a seeded table with mixed units where the first component is just the largest-variance feature; **correlation-matrix PCA** as the remedy — and when it is not appropriate, `JUDGEMENT`); **whitening** (PCA whitening `Z Λ^{-1/2}`; ZCA; with the **regularisation `ε`** for tiny eigenvalues; the resulting covariance is `I` — verified; **a note that the Cholesky route gives a different whitening**; link Unit 5); **the dual/Gram trick for `n < d`** (use the `n×n` matrix `XcXcᵀ`; derived); **the sign ambiguity and the canonical sign rule**; **PCA as a pipeline component (the MLOps lens):** **fit on the training split only**, persist `mean`, `components`, `explained_variance` as versioned artifacts, apply `transform` consistently, monitor reconstruction error as a drift signal (original synthetic demonstration of **leakage from fitting before the split**, with the measured optimistic bias and its spread); **PCA on an embedding table** (original synthetic, `[ILLUSTRATIVE]` sizes; the anisotropy and "remove the top component" ideas **named only**, `../09-…`).
- **L3:** **proofs, written in full:** (a) **the sample covariance is PSD and `S = XcᵀXc/(n−1)`** (symmetric; `vᵀSv = ‖Xc v‖²/(n−1) ≥ 0`); (b) **the first principal component maximises the projected variance**: `max_{‖w‖=1} wᵀSw = λ₁`, attained at the top eigenvector (Rayleigh quotient, Unit 6(g) or direct); (c) **the top-`k` subspace maximises the total projected variance** `tr(WᵀSW)` over orthonormal `W ∈ ℝ^{d×k}` (**Ky Fan maximum principle** — complete proof via the spectral decomposition, or `CITED [VERIFY]` with a numerical check, say which); (d) **minimum reconstruction error is equivalent to maximum variance** (Pythagoras: `Σ‖xᵢ‖² = Σ‖Pxᵢ‖² + Σ‖xᵢ − Pxᵢ‖²` for centred data) and the **minimal error equals `(n−1) Σ_{i>k} λᵢ`** (derive) — and equals the **Eckart–Young error for `Xc`** (**explicit link to Unit 8(g)**, `‖Xc − Xc_k‖_F² = Σ_{i>k} σᵢ²`); (e) **PCA via SVD equals PCA via eigen-decomposition of `S`** (`λᵢ = σᵢ²/(n−1)`; the right singular vectors are eigenvectors of `S`); (f) **components are uncorrelated**: the covariance of the scores is `diag(λ₁…λ_k)`; **the scores' variances are the eigenvalues**; (g) **uniqueness**: unique up to sign iff the top-`k` eigenvalues are distinct from each other and from `λ_{k+1}` (complete proof of the "if" direction; counterexample otherwise); (h) **scale non-invariance**: rescaling a feature changes the principal components (a minimal counterexample), whereas **orthogonal rotations of the data rotate the components equivariantly**; (i) **the dual trick**: non-zero eigenvalues of `XcXcᵀ/(n−1)` equal those of `S` with eigenvectors related by `XcᵀuI/σᵢ`; (j) **PCA whitening produces identity covariance** (derive); **numerical analysis**: **forming `S` squares the condition number — prefer the SVD of `Xc`** (measured on seeded data with a small trailing variance; error vs exact on a small rational example); float32 effects; centring errors on data with a large mean (**catastrophic cancellation when computing variance naively; two-pass vs one-pass (Welford) named `CITED [VERIFY]`**, with a measured example); **tool card** for `lalab.pca` (`fit` stores mean/components/singular values/ddof; `transform`, `inverse_transform`, explained variance, whitening with `ε`, canonical sign rule, refusal to run without centring unless `center=False` is explicit and logged; exact `Fraction` covariance/eigen oracle on tiny data; optional scikit-learn as a *non-required* oracle; stable errors).
- **L4:** **PCA's limits** (variance ≠ importance; sensitivity to outliers; linear only; scale dependence; PC interpretability claims are `JUDGEMENT`; a synthetic dataset where the discriminative direction has *low* variance and PCA discards it — original reproduction); **robust and incremental PCA named** (`[VERIFY]`; no theory; a streaming mean/covariance update demonstrated with honest limits); **kernel PCA and probabilistic PCA named only** (`../05-…`); **many features, few samples (`d ≫ n`)** (rank ≤ `n − 1`; spurious high explained variance; a seeded null experiment with spread); **explained-variance ratios on noise** (the largest eigenvalue of a pure-noise sample covariance exceeds the true variance — a measured finite-sample effect; the random-matrix theory behind it **named only**, `[VERIFY]`); **reproducibility** (sign flips across runs/platforms; **tests via principal angles**); **float32 PCA** (measured); hostile inputs (constant feature ⇒ zero variance and division by zero in standardisation; NaN; `n = 1`; `n < d`; duplicated rows); **exhaustive verification** over all `4×2` integer datasets with entries in `{−1, 0, 1}` (non-degenerate ones) of the covariance, eigenvalues, and the SVD/eigen equivalence against `sympy` (domain named and sized; `EVIDENCE ONLY`); mutation lab (centring skipped; `ddof` wrong; `V` vs `Vh`; `σ` vs `σ²` in explained variance; fit-on-all-data leakage; sort order) with no surviving mutants; case study: a model whose offline score was inflated because PCA was fit before the split (original synthetic reproduction with the measured bias, the pipeline fix, and a leakage test).
