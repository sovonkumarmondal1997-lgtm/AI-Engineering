- **Claims policy.** Every claim about learning efficacy, study method, hiring, interviews, performance, library internals, optimiser behaviour in real training, or role requirements is exactly one of: (a) cited research/documentation tagged `[VERIFY]` with limits; (b) practitioner opinion tagged `[JUDGEMENT]`; (c) a verifiable statement about mathematics, code, or the tree; or (d) omitted. **No success rates, no employer claims, no invented quotes or anecdotes, no benchmark/speed/accuracy figures as facts, no hyperparameter recipe attributed to any real model.** Durations and illustrative sizes are `[ASSUMPTION]`/`[ILLUSTRATIVE]`; operation and memory counts are **derived**.
- **Theory–practice gap policy (central to this module).** Every convergence or optimality statement is labelled with **its assumption set** (convex / `μ`-strongly convex / `L`-smooth / Polyak–Łojasiewicz / bounded gradient variance / `C²` / Lipschitz Hessian) **and its measure** (`f(xₖ) − f*`, `‖xₖ − x*‖²`, `‖∇f(xₖ)‖²`, expectation or high-probability). **A result proved under convexity or on quadratics is never presented as a statement about neural-network training**; where a practice claim about deep learning is made it is `CITED [VERIFY]` with limits or `JUDGEMENT`, and **toy experiments are labelled "toy; evidence about this problem only"**.

Agreement on examples is evidence, not proof; say so.
- **Proof policy.** Where Section 6 says "proof", write it **in full**: statement, **assumption set**, every step justified, and a closing "where each assumption was used". Where a result is `CITED [VERIFY]`, say which and give a numerical sanity check. **Rate theorems are accompanied by an instrumented experiment that checks the measured sequence stays below the proved bound (to a stated tolerance) and reports how tight the bound is.** A counterexample is a disproof; agreement on examples is not a proof.
- **Rate-verification policy.** A convergence rate is **measured** (not asserted) by fitting `log` of the error against `k` (linear rate) or `log k` (sublinear rate) **after discarding a stated transient and the round-off floor**; fits report slope, intercept, `R²`, and the fit window; **the measured contraction factor is compared to the proved factor**; seeds, repeats, and spread are reported for stochastic runs (`HEURISTIC`).
